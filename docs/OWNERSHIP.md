# Ownership: families, states and large investors

## Why ownership is a variable, not a story

A forecast that treats the firm as a black box with a production function will miss the one
thing that decides whether capacity is closed, whether a merger happens and whether a plant
survives an election. Ownership determines the time horizon. A family holds for generations, a
state holds for employment, an asset manager holds for the quarter, and a founder holds until
he decides otherwise. Each horizon produces a different response to the same loss.

The dissertation therefore codes ownership as a variable with three values, family, state and
institutional, plus a mixed category, and uses it in two places: as a control in the investment
regressions, and as a filter in the case selection.

The family cases share one mechanism: a small economic stake can control a large company
through dual class shares or a pyramid. That is why the family layer survives dilution. It also
explains why family firms close plants more slowly than asset managers would: the closure is a
social event in the region where the family name is printed on the factory gate.

## 2. States

| State | Company | Instrument | Reported position | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lower Saxony | Volkswagen AG | common shares, blocking minority | 20.0 percent of votes at 31 December 2025 | verified, Volkswagen Group Annual Report 2025, shareholder structure, accessed 24 November 2026 |
| Qatar | Volkswagen AG | common shares through Qatar Holding LLC | 17.0 percent of votes at 31 December 2025 | verified, Volkswagen Group Annual Report 2025, shareholder structure, accessed 24 November 2026 |
| France | Renault | direct state holding | about 15 percent | to verify from the French state shareholding report |
| China | SAIC, FAW, Dongfeng, Changan | wholly state owned, joint venture partners | 50 percent of each joint venture | structural, from the joint venture contracts |
| Germany | KfW and state banks | credit and guarantees | case by case | to verify per programme |

Two mechanisms matter. The first is the blocking minority, which in the German case turns 20
percent of votes into a veto over plant closures and takeovers. The second is the joint venture
rule in China, which made the state a partner in every foreign car sold there for three decades
and gave the partner half of the profit without half of the capital risk. Both are ownership
facts with direct consequences for capacity and for cash.

The institutional layer is where the time horizon shortens. An index fund cannot sell a plant,
but it can vote against a capital increase, and it sets the discount rate that decides whether a
factory is worth keeping. The dissertation treats the institutional share of the register as a
proxy for the discount rate applied to irreversible investment.

## 4. How ownership enters the model

Three channels, each measurable.

1. Investment horizon. A family or state holder with a blocking minority delays closure. Test:
   time from first loss to plant closure, by ownership type, across the cases in the register.
2. Cost of capital. A register dominated by index funds has a lower dispersion of beliefs and a
   higher sensitivity to reported earnings. Test: earnings announcement reaction by ownership
   concentration.
3. Political protection. A state stake converts a closure into an election issue. Test: the
   probability of closure in the two years before and after a regional election, controlling
   for plant age and capacity utilisation.

## 5. What must be verified before use

Every percentage in this file is a working figure. None of them enters the dissertation until
it is read from the annual report, the voting rights notification or the shareholder structure
page of the company, and recorded in the manifest with the date. The rule is the same as for
every other number in the project: a remembered number is a hypothesis, not a fact.
