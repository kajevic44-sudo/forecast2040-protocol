# The twelve papers

Each paper stands alone, with its own abstract, method, results and reference
list. They are numbered in the order of the argument, not in the order of
writing. Total length is about 30,600 words. All twelve are in English, with no
dash characters and no emphasis asterisks, as the submission rules require.

| File | Title | Target journal | What it settles |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01_forecast_protocol.md | Fixing the model before the backtest: a pre-registered protocol with a machine-checked split | International Journal of Forecasting | The selection rule is fixed before the backtest, and the split is proved in Lean, so no result can be chosen after the fact. |
| P02_battery_lca_vintage.md | Data vintage in traction battery life cycle assessment | Applied Energy | A seven-year-old impact estimate overstates current impact by about a factor of two, so battery numbers carry a date. |
| P03_used_vehicles.md | The second life of the German car | Transportation Research Part D | The used car export is a term in the balance of the new car market, not a residual. |
| P04_china_certification.md | Crossing the Wall: certification, vehicle data and asymmetric regulation | Regulation and Governance | The barrier that fell was private certification, and the data question is a three level problem: obligation, capability, occurrence. |
| P05_divestment_event_study.md | Pricing the sale of engineering businesses | Finance Research Letters | A pre-specified event study design for three disposals, with its own power limits stated in advance. |

## How the papers connect

P01 is the method that every other paper inherits: a rule fixed before the
test. P10 and P07 carry the ownership and capital layer. P02, P03 and P06 carry
the physical and labour layer. P04 and P11 carry the regulatory layer. P05,
P08, P09 and P12 carry the market, household, migration and political layers.
The synthesis at the end of the dissertation uses one result from each paper and
does not repeat their proofs.

## House rules applied to every file

1. Author is Armin Kajevic only.
2. No em dash and no en dash anywhere, ranges use the word "to".
3. No asterisks for emphasis.
4. Every number traces to a file in the repository or to arithmetic stated in
   the text.
5. Sources are named with access dates where the repository records them.
6. No sentence claims causality that the design cannot support.
